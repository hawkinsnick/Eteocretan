# Roadmap

0.1: rights and discovery inventory. 0.5: reading versions, source evidence
and exclusion boundaries. 0.9: deterministic exports and negative integrity
tests. 1.0: published attributed archive and researcher workflow.

Next: primary Dreros acquisition, modern critical-edition reconciliation,
line-level historical collation with precise uncertainty spans, Azoria
item inventory and independent review. Only then admit a documented
analytical subset. Every coverage increase requires a discovery reconciliation;
a version number does not establish exhaustive coverage or decipherment.

## Machine-verifiable gate status

The 1.0 corpus now regression-tests the zero-admission boundary, coverage-register incompleteness, disputed/uncertain object exclusions, status counts, and the expert-review queue. These tests deliberately make it harder for future edits to convert discovery leads or secondary readings into analytical evidence by accident.
