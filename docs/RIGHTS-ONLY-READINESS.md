# Linear A parity / rights-only readiness

Eteocretan is maintained toward the same evidence-control standard as Linear A, while respecting the much smaller and more disputed evidence base.

## Machine-resolvable controls now present
- attributed reading records and explicit zero-admission analytical gate;
- coverage register and source-critical dossier;
- source-lineage register preventing derivative versions from masquerading as independent witnesses;
- explicit disagreement register;
- source/rights matrix;
- residual blocker ledger;
- offline rights-allowlisted corpus browser;
- read-only research API;
- loss-aware JSON/JSONL/CSV exporter with manifest;
- pre-expert parity validator;
- expert-review queue and reproducible source audits.

## Scientific boundary
Parity means parity of method, provenance, uncertainty, rights discipline, reproducibility and reviewer readiness. It does not mean equal inscription counts, decipherment, or a completed critical edition.

Praisos 4–5 retain the project classification conflict rather than silently selecting a winner. Azoria collection counts are discovery denominators, not Eteocretan text counts. Alternative editions/readings are not independent ancient witnesses.

## Residual evidence-growth gates
Direct systematic collation of Duhoux 1982, Guarducci 1942 and complete Dreros primary evidence requires lawful access and a rights determination sufficient for the intended structured/public derivative use. Azoria object-level expansion must likewise remain at attributed public metadata until item identity and reuse rights support more.

Expert adjudication remains a later scientific gate after admissible evidence exists; it is not unfinished corpus engineering.
